Study

I hold a BS in Nutritional Science from Michigan State University  (MI, USA), and an MS in Management in Entrepreneurial Leadership from Babson College  (MA, USA).

Experience

As a former instructor at the American University in the Emirates , I taught more than 1,000 undergraduates, most of them Emirati, hands-on design thinking and practical business-building skills. Many have gone on to build impact in their local communities and support local Emirati businesses. I also spoke on the Future of EduTech panel at the Future Innovation Summit  in Dubai in 2025.

Approach

My daily work spans value proposition design, user interviews, prototyping and synthesis, and I use Claude Code , Figma  and Miro  to bring ideas to life. I'm invested in collaborating with and building alongside others locally, creating solutions born in this region. My favorite tool, though, is still pen and paper .
